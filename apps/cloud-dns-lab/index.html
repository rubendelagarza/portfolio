Lab: Migrate a zone to Cloud DNS and enable DNSSEC

Move contoso-shop.com off its legacy DNS host with zero downtime. Fix a broken zone export, lock down certificate issuance with CAA, verify Cloud DNS before cutover, switch the delegation, then sign the zone and publish the DS record in the right order.

Advanced~12 mingcloud · dig · registrar3 randomized zone exports
What this demonstrates: operating Cloud DNS, including DNSSEC: zone-file hygiene, CAA, pre-cutover verification and the DNSSEC chain of trust, where the order of steps decides whether a domain stays online.
