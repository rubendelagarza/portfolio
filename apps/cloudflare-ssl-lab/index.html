Lab: HTTPS with a Cloudflare origin certificate

Put the site behind Cloudflare and encrypt the whole path: edge certificate for visitors, a Cloudflare Origin CA certificate on nginx, and SSL mode Full (strict). Avoid the redirect loops and 52x errors along the way.

Intermediate~12 minCloudflare · nginx · openssl3 randomized starting pointsPart 2 of 3
What this demonstrates: end-to-end TLS behind a CDN: proxied DNS, Cloudflare SSL modes, origin certificates and private-key handling on Linux, and locking an origin down to the CDN's IP ranges. Continues from Lab 13, where the app is already running behind nginx.
