Lab: Fix a broken Kubernetes rollout

A change to the payments service left its rollout stuck: the new pod won't become ready while the old ones keep serving. Find the failing pod, read its events and logs, fix the cause in the manifests and finish the rollout.

Intermediate~12 minkubectl · Deployments · ConfigMaps · Secrets · probes3 randomized failures
What this demonstrates: the kubectl troubleshooting loop (get, describe, events, logs --previous), reading pod states (CrashLoopBackOff, CreateContainerConfigError, Running but not Ready), how rolling updates protect you while a new version fails, and fixing through the manifests with kubectl apply.

Alert from monitoring

You're on the ops jump host with kubectl pointed at cluster prod-mx. Manifests live in ~/k8s (a git checkout): change them with nano and kubectl apply -f.
