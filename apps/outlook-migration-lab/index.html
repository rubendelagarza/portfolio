Lab: Migrate Outlook accounts to Microsoft 365

Move five mailboxes from the old IMAP host to Exchange Online with a migration batch, cut over at the right moment, then get Outlook on a user's PC onto Exchange Online with her archive and shared mailbox, without losing a thing.

Intermediate~15 minPowerShell (EXO) · Outlook · M365 admin center3 randomized starting points
What this demonstrates: the Microsoft 365 post-migration work I do at Perficient: migration endpoints and batches, reading migration errors, licensing prerequisites, cutover timing, and the Outlook side that generates most tickets (profiles, Autodiscover, PST imports, shared mailbox auto-mapping).
